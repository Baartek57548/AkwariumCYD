CYD (Cheap Yellow Display) Modular Case by DanielBrunod on Thingiverse: https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:6991968

Summary:
This is a modular rework / remix of the original case design by ncmreynolds, licensed under the Creative Commons - Attribution - Share Alike license. Full credit to them for the original idea and base structure.What’s New?The goal of this remix was to simplify and modularize the case:Simplified Top and Bottom LayersThe top and bottom sections are now smaller and less complex, making them easier and faster to print.Better Fit for CYD BoardsThe original groove was too tight for some CYD boards. This version adjusts that for a more reliable fit.Modular ExtensionsYou can now stack layers to suit your project. Add or remove sections as needed!Included FilesCYDEnclosureRear.stlUpdated with an additional hole to allow easy access to the pen reset and reboot buttons.CYDEnclosureFront.stlA slimmer, simplified front case with reduced wall height and openings for both MicroUSB and USB-C ports.CYDProtoLayer.stlA 1.5mm thick layer featuring a grid of 3mm holes – perfect for mounting add-on boards like GPS, NRF24, etc.CYDProtoLayerSpeakerHole.stlA 1mm thick layer featuring a grid of 3mm holes, with an opening for the speaker connector, to be mounted close to the frontbox – perfect for mounting add-on boards like GPS, NRF24, etc.CYDEnclosureExtender15mm.stlA 15mm intermediate layer with two SMA connector holes .CYDEnclosureExtender10mm.stlA clean 10mm layer with no holes – great for adding height or modifying to suit your own needs. This layer scales well and is ready for customization.CYDEnclosureExtender06mm.stlA clean 06mm layer with no holes – great for adding height or modifying to suit your own needs. This layer scales well and is ready for customization.CYD_PenHolder06mm.stlA 06mm clean layer with a pen holder for the original pen that comes with the CYD. ******All exterior holes are designed for M3 screws.**** Size depends on your stack!
